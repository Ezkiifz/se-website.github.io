Activity

Software Engineering Sport is an activity that is held once a month and usually held at the weekend cause at the weekend is the best time for students to enjoy the holidays. This activity is very enjoyable cause every student not only enjoy the holidays but also students get to exercise to maintain their health. In this activity, not only is the only one sport that is done once a month, but there are many sports in activity, such as futsal, football on the mini soccer field, basketball, volleyball, and badminton.

Software Engineering Games is an activity that is held once a year and usually held at the end of odd semester cause at this time is the best time for students to enjoy the day at school to avoid feeling bored when the material in that semester is finished and approaching the odd semester break. This activity is very enjoyable cause every student not only enjoy the day at school. They also get to compete against other classes in both individual and team categories to win prizes for various video games.

Software Engineering League is an activity that is held once a year and usually held at the end of even semester cause at this time is the best time for students to enjoy the day at school to avoid feeling bored when the material in that semester is finished and approaching the even semester break. This activity is very enjoyable cause every student not only enjoy the day at school. They also get to compete against other classes in both individual and team categories to win prizes for various video games and sports.
